## 6 Evaluation

This chapter evaluates the artifacts presented in [Chapters 3](../03_Logistics_Equipment_Assemblies/03_Logistics_Equipment_Assemblies.md#3-mtp-based-automation-of-logistics-equipment-assemblies) through [5](../05_Logistics_Area/05_Logistics_Area.md#5-mtp-based-automation-of-flexible-transport-in-logistics-areas). Following the Design Science Research methodology in [[PTR+07]](../08_References/README.md#peffers-et-al-2007), the applicability of the artifacts is first demonstrated through three concrete evaluation examples ([Section 6.1](06-01_EvaluationExamples.md)). Building on these, [Section 6.2](06-02_Verification.md) verifies the artifacts against the requirements defined in [Section 2.6](../02_Modular_Logistics_System/02_Modular_Logistics_System.md#26-automation-requirements). Finally, [Section 6.3](06-03_Validation.md) validates the usefulness of the artifacts in the problem context and their suitability to fulfill the research goal.

The following sections describe the individual evaluation steps.

- [Section 6.1 — Evaluation Examples](./06-01_EvaluationExamples.md): Three prototypical implementations — a bottle-filling Logistics Line at BASF SE, a palletizing Logistics Area at BEUMER Group, and a PLC-based emulation of a comprehensive MLS — are investigated as controlled experiments to demonstrate the practical applicability of the artifacts.
- [Section 6.2 — Verification](./06-02_Verification.md): The artifacts are verified against the automation requirements defined in [Section 2.6](../02_Modular_Logistics_System/02_Modular_Logistics_System.md#26-automation-requirements).
- [Section 6.3 — Validation](./06-03_Validation.md): The usefulness of the artifacts in the problem context and their suitability to fulfill the research goal are assessed.
